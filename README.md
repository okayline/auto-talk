# auto-talk

A street of 10 planet neighbours, 5 facing 5. You pick who lives on your side. The street runs on your real clock: each neighbour sleeps at their personality's hours, and when awake is home or out on the road. Neighbours who are out meet, shown with emote bubbles; how it goes depends on their personality types. After a clash, one of them goes home to think for a while.

Plain HTML and JavaScript, no dependencies. Open `index.html` through any local web server (it loads `sim.js` as a module). Tests: `npm test`.
