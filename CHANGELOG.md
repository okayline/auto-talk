# Changelog

## v0.8 (Nov 19, 2026)
- Added: a New game button that opens a popup with the 10 planets and your 5 empty houses. Drag a planet into a house, or click a planet then a house; click a filled house to empty it. Move in closes the popup once all 5 are filled.
- Removed: the checkboxes at the top.

## v0.7 (Nov 19, 2026)
- Changed: the road is split into spots with one neighbour per spot, so wanderers no longer pile up or walk through two who are talking.
- Removed: wandering to any point on the road.

## v0.6 (Nov 19, 2026)
- Added: neighbours who are out wander all over the road, stepping to a new spot every few seconds. Two who are meeting walk together and trade speech bubbles, then show how it ended. Others out on the road now and then show a small emote.
- Removed: the list of meetings under the street, and standing only in front of their own house.

## v0.5 (Nov 19, 2026)
- Changed: a meeting now ends happy, sad or angry, as villager talks do in Animal Crossing.
- Removed: rules that weren't from Animal Crossing: the meeting labels, going home to think after an angry talk, the rest between two neighbours meeting again, and the guessed sleep hours for SLY and TSUNDERE (they stay awake until the real hours are found).

## v0.4 (Nov 19, 2026)
- Added: the street runs on your real clock. Neighbours sleep at their personality's hours, and when awake are home (lit window) or out on the road, decided each hour. Neighbours who are out meet every minute or two, with emote bubbles and a log of this hour's meetings. After a clash one of the two goes home to think for a while, and a pair who just met don't meet again straight away. The page updates itself.
- Removed: the "Play the day" and "Next day" buttons and the once-a-day run of 10 meetings.

## v0.3 (Nov 19, 2026)
- Added: two more neighbours, Pluto (GRUFF) and the Moon (DREAMY), so all 10 houses are filled: you pick 5 of 10 for your side and the other 5 live across the street.
- Removed: the empty houses.

## v0.2 (Nov 19, 2026)
- Added: a two-sided street: 5 houses across the road from your 5, facing each other in pairs. Across the street are the planets you didn't pick, and empty houses for the rest. They don't take part in the day yet.
- Removed: the single row of houses.

## v0.1 (Nov 19, 2026)
- Added: a street of 5 houses, a choice of 5 from 8 planet neighbours (placeholder round heads), a day of 10 meetings shown one at a time with emote bubbles and the reason, neighbours who clashed staying in the next day, a "Next day" button, and tests for the day logic.
- Removed: nothing (first version).
